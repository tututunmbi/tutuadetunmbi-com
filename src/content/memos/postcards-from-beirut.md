I’ve always been a bit of a homebody. Even more so in the last 2 years. My comfort zone is a cosy little bubble that I am always reluctant to burst. But sometimes, the most extraordinary experiences happen when you step outside of that bubble. And that’s exactly what I did when I decided to take a trip to Beirut.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/ac38916f-cf12-4ee9-8a8f-619c405fb917_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!uQTe!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fac38916f-cf12-4ee9-8a8f-619c405fb917_3024x4032.heic)

It was a spontaneous decision, a crazy idea that I didn’t think I would have the balls to follow through but I have had this tug for a while to experience something new. I had little to no idea what to expect, but I was filled with a mix of excitement and trepidation. Western media did not help in any way.

I couldn’t have imagined a more fun, light and interesting week!

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/564e75c5-d6f8-481d-88a8-9a40d1fdcf96_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7ZNd!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F564e75c5-d6f8-481d-88a8-9a40d1fdcf96_3024x4032.heic)

Beirut has my heart! It’s ancient history mixed with modern culture, I couldn’t stop taking pictures of the stunning architecture. As a cloud chaser, the views are something else (the sunsets…omg!) and the city knows how to party! One of the most rewarding aspects of my trip was the people I met, they made this trip one I won’t forget.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/f8da01ad-191b-4a03-be60-d3c0dcd92d28_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KhEq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff8da01ad-191b-4a03-be60-d3c0dcd92d28_3024x4032.heic)

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/f3c10428-6e38-440b-9a1f-7f580d72cd86_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!agFk!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3c10428-6e38-440b-9a1f-7f580d72cd86_3024x4032.heic)

Stepping outside of my comfort zone was a tad bit challenging at times, but it was also incredibly liberating. I tried new foods, danced to Lebanese music(which bangs btw!), and even jumped off a cliff (Yeah, literally…I went paragliding).

I had an incredible time! It has shown me that there is so much more to see, do, and experience. And I'm determined to make the most of it.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/89532f97-856a-44ba-8914-9b0750e66271_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CdUe!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89532f97-856a-44ba-8914-9b0750e66271_3024x4032.heic)

It’s forced me to strictly make plans to travel at least once a year. I'm also considering working remotely in another country for a month or two. The world is my oyster, and I'm eager to explore as much of it as I can.

Beirut was just the beginning of my journey of self-discovery. I'm excited to see where my adventures will take me next.
