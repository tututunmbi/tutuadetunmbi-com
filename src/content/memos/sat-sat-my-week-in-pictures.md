I had the amazing opportunity to speak on a career exploration panel at Navigating the Next, an incredible event hosted by Kennedy Ekezie at the University of Lagos. I live and breathe social media strategy and brand storytelling. So, my mission was to tell my story. Events like this light me up. I've walked this path with limited guidance and blindfolded, and if sharing my story can help others see more clearly, well, that's an immense reward.

### SUNDAY

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/a7bafa67-3b54-4b8f-b9dd-62a853f633ff_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Im4J!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa7bafa67-3b54-4b8f-b9dd-62a853f633ff_3024x4032.heic)

My Sundays are non-negotiable. I have to get myself right for the week ahead and nothing…and I mean nothing comes between me and my Sundays. I reflect on how the week went, I guage my feelings, my intentions and things that went really well and not so great. I take a look at my weekly goals and review what I got done and what I failed to accomplish. I give myself a performance percentage and map out next week’s goals and intentions. I also set a theme for the week, like a mindset I want to adopt or focus on for that week.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8191a052-d308-45fe-9876-81a047d5f18e_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!BN45!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8191a052-d308-45fe-9876-81a047d5f18e_3024x4032.heic)

### TUESDAY

Tuesday I woke up fully clothed, makeup on, and phone in my hand. I went out for drinks last night and must have passed out…😬

### WEDNESDAY

Today was the imaginary public holiday, and I was in a meeting throughout. I have been working on a really important project. I have been building a tool. I guess I am about to launch a startup and it is going to be centered around digital professionals.

It has been over a year for me to pitch, ideate, go to school, build the features and functionalites and learn about the tech industry, their lingo, and how (crazy!) they are.

But we now are in the design and development stage!!! I can see what it is going to look like and my heart started to beat! This meeting was them telling me that it is time to start talking about it, to let the world know and to fully step into the role as Founder. I started to shake, this means I have big life decisions to make.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/d3df469e-a557-4e5f-b216-6dd436d5035b_3024x4032.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t0NT!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd3df469e-a557-4e5f-b216-6dd436d5035b_3024x4032.heic)

The Yoruba in me says I should wait until the app is ready then announce, but I was told that’s not how things work in tech, and I have to start sharing my story, the journey to building and developing it, have a roll out plan and build a waitlist.

*Internal heart palpitations*

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/25cb2a4e-6083-47c3-88a0-acd5cf5b46e6_1284x2282.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Qwfq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F25cb2a4e-6083-47c3-88a0-acd5cf5b46e6_1284x2282.heic)

This was a meeting that shook up my week.

But I was ready. This is something I have nurtured for a year, working on it, learning about things…I am more than ready to share this with the world and share my journey to building this idea.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/77b42e21-b067-44e3-9ab3-7cd780e5875a_1152x1255.heic)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!nM7e!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F77b42e21-b067-44e3-9ab3-7cd780e5875a_1152x1255.heic)
