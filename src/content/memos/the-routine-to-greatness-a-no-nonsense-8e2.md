### **STEP 5: Set the Standards for Your Life**

Now that you've envisioned your Final Form, this next part might seem like a breeze. But here's where many stumble. They dive headfirst into work, the gym, and goal-chasing – all without a crucial foundation.

Before you launch yourself into action, let's talk about the single most important factor in staying on track: your standards. They're the architects of your habits. Think of them as the bar you set for yourself – the minimum you'll accept in any area of your life, be it career, relationships, health, or mental well-being.

You need a clear vision of "***this is what I expect***" for each aspect of your life. **Your standards will dictate the daily habits you cultivate to meet them**, ultimately propelling you toward your goals.

Remember, everything starts with you. A shift in mindset triggers a shift in standards, which then impacts your habits. You simply won't tolerate things that don't align with your desired reality.

Feeling me? So, ask yourself: what's the bare minimum you accept in your appearance, finances, work ethic, discipline, and consistency? What standards have you set for yourself?

---

### **STEP 6: Craft your Final Form Routine**

Alright, you have done great so far getting here. I am so proud of you! Your journey to greatness ends here, in developing your final form routine. Remember developing the vision of your final form? The one you crafted in vivid detail – the embodiment of your ultimate success? Now, get ready to turn that vision into a tangible reality. We'll create a daily routine that reflects that Final Form – not your current reality, but your destiny!

**Imagine your ideal week: Map out your ideal schedule, Monday through Sunday.** Be as detailed as possible!

- **What time do you wake up in your Final Form world?** What morning rituals do you perform to set the tone for the day?
- **How do you structure your workday for peak productivity and fulfilment?** Are there specific work habits or routines that empower your Final Form self?
- **How many times a week do you exercise?** What kind of physical activity fuels your ideal Final Form?
- **Do you carve out time for hobbies and passions?** What activities spark joy and ignite your creativity in your Final Form life?
- **Do you have any non-negotiable rituals, weekly, monthly, or quarterly?** Maybe it's a meditation, beauty maintenance, brunch with friends, church, monthly reviews, or a spa day?

**Remember, this isn't about perfection, it's about inspiration.** You might not be able to afford a personal trainer right now, but your Final Form routine might include a daily morning workout. Can't afford a fancy co-working space yet? Your Final Form might include setting up a dedicated workspace at home to maximize your focus.

**Start with what you have, and bridge the gap:**

- **Can't afford a gym?** Find free home workout videos online.
- **Longing to learn a new skill?** Explore free online courses or YouTube resources.
- **Dreaming of international travel?** Start by researching foreign language learning apps or connecting with cultural communities online.

**The key is to take consistent action, no matter how small, to move closer to your Final Form routine.** Every step, every action, becomes a building block towards your ultimate success.

You are the sum of your routines and habits. This is what keeps you consistent, this is what keeps you disciplined. And we all know the quote right? *consistency will outperform talent any single day*. I want you to get to that stage, taking one step every day, doing one thing that will change your life. That's all you need, consistency and discipline and that comes from knowing what you are doing Monday to Sunday, at every particular point in time.

Greatness isn't about magical moments or strokes of luck. It's about the consistent effort of building and following routines and habits that propel you forward, even when they feel challenging.

F.M. Alexander hits the nail on the head about routines:

"Success is accomplished by habitually doing things that people who have never succeeded find difficult to do."
