In a world often clouded by self-doubt and uncertainty, it's easy to overlook the profound impact we can have on others' lives. Yet, within each of us lies the potential to be someone's answered prayer. I am just here to share with you that your existence, your journey, is not merely happenstance; it's a divine orchestration, a cosmic alignment of purpose waiting to unfold.

I can't help but reflect on the story of Moses, a man who unknowingly walked the path of destiny. He could have lived a life of privilege and comfort, basking in the riches that came with being adopted by Pharaoh's daughter and raised as an Egyptian. But destiny had other plans. The Israelites, as it says in the bible, "groaned in their slavery and cried out, and their cry for help went up to God”... Moses, basking in privilege, was (if you look at it humorously, unfortunately) called to be a deliverer, a beacon of hope for his people. Despite the trials and tribulations he faced, Moses embraced his purpose with unwavering courage and faith. Though at first, he was nervous, stubborn, and sometimes angry at the cross he had to bear.

This message is for anyone who finds themselves at a crossroads, you may be hesitant to embrace the call to greatness within you. Perhaps fear holds you back, whispering doubts and insecurities. But know this: the very thing you're afraid to do may be the key to unlocking someone else's liberation. Your message, your voice, has the power to inspire, to heal, to teach, to warn, to uplift.

You are here to deliver someone or something that is groaning and crying out for help. No matter how small you may believe that thing to be.

So, I know you wish your journey in life could've been easier. I know you may be nervous, stubborn, and sometimes angry at the things you had to experience in your own journey but it is time to get up and embrace your role, there are souls out there longing for the light only you can bring. That light is the message, the perception and the lessons that only you can take from those experiences that make you angry, nervous and stubborn.

So, what's your message? What truth burns within your soul, waiting to be shared with the world? Trust in the power of your voice, for you are indeed someone's answered prayer.
