We are in the middle of a shift…a divine recalibration that’s returning power to those who think differently, who create intentionally, who are no longer willing to live in reaction. The age of passive existence is over. A new kind of leader is rising. Not the loudest, not the most popular, but the most *attuned*.

There are people right now who are destined to change the way we see the world. But they are dormant. Not because they lack the power, but because they are afraid.

In a world where everything can be replicated, your taste, your perspective, your essence, becomes your most valuable asset.

The day I stopped explaining myself to people who had no capacity to understand me was the day I reclaimed my power. Somewhere along the way, someone convinced you that you are unworthy, even as you journey toward worthiness.

But I’m here to remind you: you are not here to merely exist.

And no, your purpose is not something outside of you. It is not something to find. It is something to *become.*

The life I’ve lived,  
The experiences I’ve had,  
The insights I’ve earned,  
The mindset I’ve cultivated.  
It was all soulfully curated. Not just for me to survive, but for me to **share**.

I share it all because I know someone out there needs my *exact* story  
So they too can find the courage to share theirs.  
And the collective expands.

We all carry different energies.  
In a crisis, a butterfly doesn’t become a lion.  
We are all here to *be who we are*.  
To lend our hands, our hearts, our minds, our spirits,   
*As we are designed to.*

I know who I am because I know my whole story.  
Every line. Every chapter. Every pause.  
It all shaped the synopsis of me.

It took time.  
But that’s okay.  
It *had* to.

I am an *interesting book.*  
And the best ones take time to write.

We’ve inherited a world that conditioned us to conform in order to survive. And while those rules are fading, what’s left behind is a generation grappling with confusion, lost in their own identities, unsure how to exist outside the boxes they were born into.

To know yourself, to like yourself, to move with your own rhythm…that’s a luxury these days.

But it shouldn’t be.

Because if you don’t know who you are, how can you ever discover your purpose? And if you’ve never truly *met yourself*, how can you walk in the direction of your calling with clarity or confidence?

Let me tell you something radical:

> **You are the purpose.**  
> The thing you’re desperately searching for is already inside you.  
> And no one else can live it for you.

You are a *living message*, a walking, breathing epistle. You were *sent.* Your life is not an accident. Your story…all of it… is sacred literature written in real-time. Your heartbreaks, your patterns, your power…none of it is wasted.

You are made in the image and likeness of God. Not metaphorically. Literally. Billions of us, each one sent here to reflect a unique facet of the divine.

Your purpose is not always a career. Sometimes it’s a vibration. A mindset. A movement. A new way of seeing. A different way of doing. A shift you model for the world.

And the more you align with the truth of who you are, the more magnetic your life becomes.

You want to know your purpose?

Start here:

> Who are you?  
> No, really. *Who are you?*

Strip away the roles. The titles. The fears. The masks. Who are you when no one is watching?

Most people you meet are living as someone else. Trying to mimic what looks like success. Trying to earn belonging through performance. But the real power begins when you stop auditioning for a life that’s not yours.

It’s the struggle.  
It’s the wrestling.  
It’s the pulling.  
It’s the trial.  
It’s the temptation.  
It’s the stumbling, the falling, the rising again.  
It’s the aggravation. The intimidation.  
*That*…all of that…is the foundation of everything you “*one day become.*”

We are all here carrying fragments of God. His feelings. His mind. His mercy. His anger. His compassion. His creativity. His joy. You are not meant to blend in. You were born to *reveal* something. A thought. A shift. A way.

And no…it’s not about being perfect.

---

You care too much about what people think. What they’ll say. How you’ll look. If you’re ready. If you’re polished enough. And I get it. But ***rest*****.** There are people *dreaming*, *waiting*, *praying* for the thing that only you can bring.

You are the missing piece. You are the key.  
*Why are you keeping people waiting?*

Why does your heart feel like it wants to leap out of your chest when you consider sharing that one idea?  
Why is it that you can do *everything else* with ease,  
But your body freezes when it’s time to share *that* one post, that “Hot Take”  
That business idea,  
That book,  
That truth or project you’ve held quietly?

Let me tell you why.

Because that’s the one that carries your divine assignment.  
That’s the one your soul was sent to deliver.  
That’s the message. That’s the breakthrough.  
And the enemy of your potential will always war hardest at the gate of your becoming.

Before you build a business, write a book, or launch the next big idea, go on a journey of discovering, defining, and developing your *self-concept*. That’s the foundation. That’s your energy. That’s the root of everything. Because once you know who you are, no one can tell you otherwise.

And please, stop seeing yourself through the lens of who you used to be.  
You are not your past.  
You are not your mistakes.  
You are not your fear.

> **Every day, every season, every moment…  
> You’re being called into someone new.  
> Answer the call.**

Your job is simple: *show up.*

As you are. Authentically.  
Reluctantly, if you must.  
But still…show up.

You’ll always win when you look at the game differently.  
When the world says it’s over, choose again. You have been given something that MUST be shared.   
A champion chess player knows: if the king can move, the game is not over.  
So move. Choose. Win.

And stop vomiting your grace.  
You’ve been entrusted with something divine,   
something you *feel*.

But you keep handing it out diluted,  
hoping someone else will validate the miracle you already know it is.

Stop that.  
Let it be miraculous. Let it be holy. Let it be *yours*.

Your life is not meant to be a performance. It’s a mosaic. A body of work. A living archive of breadcrumbs that lead others toward healing, inspiration, and liberation.

Don’t get caught up in the pressure of making each post, each project, each moment perfect. Zoom out. Ask:

- *Does this reflect the larger story I want to tell?*
- *Is this part of the mosaic of my calling?*
- *Am I being faithful to my essence?*

Because the truth is: you never know which breadcrumb will change your life. The more surface area you create, through action, through art, through truth, the more likely the right people, opportunities, and connections will find you.

Your perspective is needed.  
You fear being seen…  
And yet the world craves to see itself through *your* eyes.  
Your mission might be to answer someone’s silent prayer.  
To deliver.  
To teach.  
To move someone to their own promised land.

Remember: those who develop distinct thought patterns and refined taste are building something greater than content. They are creating *worlds* others long to inhabit.

So when you resist out of fear of being seen…

Every time you hide yourself,  
Every time you shrink,  
Or hold back from your power, you underestimate the ripple effect.

You silence *possibilities* that could shift the status quo.

You’ll never know what might be awakened in the world *through you*,  
Unless you allow it.

Your mosaic may look small now… just tiles, scattered pieces. But with time, with faith, with devotion to your inner compass, a masterpiece will emerge.

Keep placing your tiles. Keep showing up.

The world needs your message.  
And that message… is **you**.

You are not missing anything.  
You are not lost.  
You are the message.

You were wired for the very thing you’re afraid to release.  
So go.  
Become.  
Break through.  
And in doing so…  
You set the rest of us free.

The new economy doesn’t demand that you do more.  
It asks that you **be more** ***you***:   
Clear.  
Intentional.  
Unshakably discerning.

…I look forward to seeing you shine.
