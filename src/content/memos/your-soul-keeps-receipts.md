You will always be stuck in cycles of diminishing returns if you don’t confront your own lack of self-worth. It trickles down into everything…your decisions, your delays, your default to smallness.

*Aren’t you tired of adjusting???*

I’ve been led to give you a brutal wake-up call. I hope you’re ready. And I’m sorry.

> One day, you’ll break down and realize the only reason you haven’t become everything you’re capable of… is because you chose performance over purpose.

It probably started small. Barely noticeable. You made yourself agreeable to the world and called it humility. You read the room and gave it what it needed. And when you saw that palatability made you more acceptable, you leaned in.

You danced to the rhythm of what they needed you to be, not who you truly are.

So you shrank… Repeatedly.

You didn’t want to be too much. Too loud. Too daring. Too *“I don’t give a fxxk who you are”*. Too ambitious. Too clear. Too convicted. So you trained your soul to contort itself for comfort, and then you called that wisdom.

…we are what we repeatedly do…

If you’ve been acting small to not scare people, you became small. If you’ve been shrinking to blend in, you disappeared. And your subconscious adjusted. It started to help you sabotage anything that looked like your next level. It made excuses. Delayed timelines. Normalized fear.

*Am I painting the picture now?…*

Even your environment adjusted. You attracted people who matched the version of you you had settled into. So how could you possibly see beyond the world you built to survive?

And your soul? It kept receipts. It whispered truths. It nudged you to grow. But you translated those nudges into delays:

*"I’m not ready.”*

*“Now’s not the time.”*

*“Let me wait just a little longer.”*

….😐😐😐…

And while you waited, the attack worked.

You started calling your hesitation strategy. You gave fear the mic and called it caution…Laying down in a cesspit of your own inner struggles of belief and disbelief. But deep down, you knew **you were the one holding the leash.**

*…have I lost you, or are you still with me..*

Sometimes, it’s not the enemy attacking your destiny. It’s **you**.

Sometimes, the weapon formed against you **is you.**

Self-deliverance is a real thing. And the performer’s path is the hardest to break. Because you’ve left parts of yourself with so many people, spread pieces of your true self across the expectations of so many people, you’ve got nothing left to anchor your truth to.

But hear me: it’s not too late. Collect yourself. Reclaim the pieces. Take the keys back.

**Stand the f**k up.

It is time to stand the fuck up!

**Do you know who the hell you are?**
